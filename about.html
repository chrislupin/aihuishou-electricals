About Aihuishou Electricals

We help homes, businesses and institutions recover value from obsolete, damaged or unwanted electronics while keeping hazardous waste out of landfills and waterways.

Our mission

We are building a more responsible electronics economy in Kenya by collecting and recovering devices, components and materials for reuse, refurbishment and safe recycling.

  • Reduce toxic e-waste in the environment
  • Recover reusable materials and components
  • Support circular supply chains for local businesses

Why it matters

Improper disposal of electronics can cause harmful chemical leakage, resource loss and community health risks. We offer a traceable route for safe and accountable handling.
