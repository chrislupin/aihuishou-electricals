About Aihuishou Electricals Limited

Aihuishou Electricals Limited is a Kenyan electronic waste recovery company focused on collecting, recovering and responsibly managing unwanted electronic equipment and components.

We help homes, businesses and institutions manage obsolete devices in a way that protects the environment, recovers valuable materials and supports a circular economy.

Our work is grounded in traceability, compliance-minded handling and practical operational support for customers who need a cleaner and more responsible electronics lifecycle.

Frequently asked questions

What electronic waste do you buy?

We buy and recover a wide range of electronic waste including motherboards, CPUs, RAM, LCD screens, batteries, laptops and mobile phones.

Do you collect e-waste from homes?

Yes. We collect from homes, offices and institutions, depending on the size and type of items.

Do you collect from businesses?

Yes. We support businesses and organizations with scheduled collections and recovery reporting.

Where are you located?

We are based in Viken Thirty, Ruai, Nairobi, Kenya, and serve customers across Nairobi and surrounding areas.

Do you buy damaged electronics?

Yes. We buy damaged, obsolete and non-working electronics that can still be recovered or processed responsibly.

Do you buy dead motherboards?

Yes. Dead or unwanted motherboards are part of our recovery stream and are assessed for material and component value.

How do I sell my e-waste?

Send us a WhatsApp message or complete the pickup form with your details and the items you have available.

Do you provide pickup services?

Yes. Pickup arrangements are available for households, businesses and institutions depending on the volume and location.

How does your agent program work?

Agents can register through our application portal and get support for collection and recovery operations as part of the network.

What happens to my electronics after collection?

Items are inspected, categorized, recovered for reuse where possible and directed to responsible recycling channels when necessary.

How do you handle devices with personal data?

We advise customers to remove personal data before pickup. Where required, devices are handled with careful sorting and secure processing practices.
